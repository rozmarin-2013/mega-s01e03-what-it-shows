Shared parser

The shared parser reads dates month-first. For tenants whose locale writes the day first, dates with two valid month numbers are silently flipped. This affects app, email, and form reports. It covers 374 reports after the desk moved five reports to separate work.

booking-v3 hint

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first tenants follow that hint, but the form reads the entered value in the tenant's order. This is a separate form problem, even when the report subject matches a parser report.

One-day moves

Five reports do not describe a day/month swap. They describe a one-day or weekday shift, an invite before its booking, or the same meeting landing on different weekdays. The desk moved them to separate work after the first review, so they need their own open ticket.

The subject line and intake tag are not enough to deduplicate these reports. Classify them by tenant locale, booking-form version, and channel.

Triage change

Keep one engineering ticket per cause. Deduplicate overlapping export rows by report ID before counting. A desk move is stronger evidence than the intake tag, so move those reports out of the parser group when the desk reclassifies them.
